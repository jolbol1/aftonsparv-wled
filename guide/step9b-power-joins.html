1. Unplug the USB lead. Undo any temporary step-7 twists.
  2. Lay the C3 and MT3608 where they'll sit, then cut their red and black wires to reach the socket wires. Don't shorten the socket wires.
  3. Join A (5 V): socket plain white + MT3608 red + C3 red. Strip 8 mm, pigtail, solder, snip to 10 mm, fold, tape.
  4. Join B (GND): socket black + MT3608 black + C3 black. Same again, and keep it apart from A.

socket plain-white lug ↔ C3 5V padbeep
socket black lug ↔ C3 G padbeep
plain-white lug ↔ black lugno beep
Then the first power-up: USB lead into the charger, C3 5V ↔ G ≈ 5 V, MT3608 VOUT ≈ 24 V. The dashed VOUT wires come next.
