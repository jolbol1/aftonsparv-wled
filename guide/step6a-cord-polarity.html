1. Unplug the IKEA adapter from the wall.
  2. Cut its lead about 10 cm from the brick. Keep the long end with the barrel plug. The brick and stub go in the bin.
  3. Pull the two conductors of the long end apart for about 3 cm, strip 5 mm off each and twist the strands.
  4. Push the barrel plug into the lamp's DC socket.
  5. Meter: dial to the diode/beep symbol and press SEL until you see the speaker icon. Touch the probes together: it should beep.
  6. One probe on the socket lug with the plain white wire, the other on A, then on B. The one that beeps is +.
The polarity doesn't depend on wire colour or stripes, so trust the beep. Tell me which one it was and how you marked it, and we'll move on to the USB cable.
