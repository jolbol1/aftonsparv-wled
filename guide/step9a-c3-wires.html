1. Tin each pad first: touch the iron to the pad for about 1 s and feed a tiny bit of solder in, so it's filled with a little dome.
  2. Tin each wire end: strip 3 mm, twist, heat and add a little solder until it's silver.
  3. Hold the tinned wire on the tinned pad, touch the iron on top for about 2 s, and let go when they fuse. No extra solder needed.
  4. Do 5V (red) and G (black) first, then 4, 5, 6 with each module's gate wire.
  5. Run the meter checks below before anything gets powered.
