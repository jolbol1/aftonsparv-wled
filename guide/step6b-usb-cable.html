1. Cut the USB cable, keeping the USB-A end with 30 cm to 1 m of cable.
  2. Strip about 3 cm of the outer jacket. Inside you'll usually find red, black, green, white, sometimes with foil and a bare wire too.
  3. Cut green, white and any foil or bare wire short, and fold them back. They're not used.
  4. Strip 5 mm off red and black and twist the strands. Keep the two bare ends apart.
  5. Meter on DC volts. Plug the USB into the charger, put the red probe on red and the black probe on black. You should read about 5.0 V.
  6. Unplug the charger.
Next is the join: red → the IKEA + conductor and black → −, soldered and insulated separately. Have you got PVC tape or heat-shrink for that?
