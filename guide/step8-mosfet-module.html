  1. Clamp the MOSFET by its tab. Bend G and S out about 30°, 3 mm below the body. Leave D straight.
  2. Wrap and solder one wire per leg: gate on G, drain on D, black source on S. Tape each leg.
  3. About 4 cm out, strip a 6 mm window in the gate and source wires and fit the 10 kΩ across them.
  4. Further along the gate wire, cut it and splice in the 100 Ω: wire → resistor → wire.
  5. Tape every joint and tag the module R, G or B. Leave the tails 10–12 cm long.
Source wires from all three modules join together later and go to the MT3608's VOUT− pad (GND).
