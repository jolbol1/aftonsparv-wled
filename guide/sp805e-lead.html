Old USB 2.0 cableWHAT WE USED

Any USB-A to micro/C lead. Cut both plugs off. It has four cores: red, black, green, white. Some cheap leads have no black and use the bare braid instead, which works fine as one of the four.

4-pin RGB strip extension

JST-SM or 4-pin LED-strip extension, rated 12/24 V. It gives you a plug, so the lamp can be unplugged from the controller.

Any 4-core signal cable

Alarm, phone or speaker cable with four cores, 24–28 AWG or thicker. Anything rated for 24 V at 0.5 A is plenty.
Don't leave USB plugs on the lead. It carries 24 V, and plugging it into a USB port would damage whatever's on the other end.
