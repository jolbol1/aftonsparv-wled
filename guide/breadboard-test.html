Check your C3's printed labels. If it's the other way round (USB-C to the right), the pins land in different columns; just follow the labels. Each column of five holes (a–e) is one connection, so the exact row doesn't matter. Only use holes the C3 doesn't cover. Current path: 5V → 220 Ω → LED → drain → source → GND.
