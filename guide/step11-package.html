1. Unplug the USB lead.
  2. Insulation sweep: every joint taped, with no copper or solder visible anywhere. Tape each MOSFET's metal tab: it's connected to drain, so two tabs touching would mix colours, and a tab touching GND would leave that colour stuck on.
  3. Tape the back of the MT3608, where its sharp solder points stick out. Don't wrap it tight round the coil; it gets slightly warm.
  4. Lay it out as above. Bend the MOSFETs flat, fold excess wire into gentle loops (no sharp kinks), and keep everything away from the screw posts so the cover screws can't pinch a wire.
  5. Keep the C3's antenna end (the red "C3" block) free of wires and metal, ideally where the cover is thinnest.
  6. Hold things in place with small strips of tape across the channel, or double-sided foam tape if you have it.
  7. Test before closing: power on, run a full colour cycle, and check the Wi-Fi signal in WLED (Info). Aim for above about 50%.
  8. Close the cover gently. If it won't sit flat, stop and find what's in the way. Never force it, because that's how wires get pinched. Screws in, sticker back on.
  9. Power on and test once more with the cover on.
Then WLED housekeeping: save a favourite colour as a preset, set it as the boot preset, and optionally add it to Home Assistant (it's auto-discovered).
