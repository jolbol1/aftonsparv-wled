1. Unplug the charger. Pull the barrel plug out of the lamp too.
  2. Stagger the joints: cut the IKEA − conductor and the USB red about 2 cm shorter than the other two, so the two joints sit 2 cm apart.
  3. Join red → IKEA + (the marked one): overlap the bare strands, twist them together tightly, and solder until the solder flows into the twist.
  4. Join black → IKEA − the same way.
  5. Tape each joint on its own once it has cooled: 3 tight turns, starting on the insulation before the joint and finishing past it. Then wrap over both joints and onto both cable jackets.
