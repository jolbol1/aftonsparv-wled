  1. Use the three tested MOSFETs. Trim each leg to about 10 mm. Keep them straight.
  2. Solder the 10 kΩ (brown-black-orange) between the G and S legs on the back (tab side), so it bridges over D without touching it.
  3. Solder the 100 Ω (brown-black-brown) to the G leg, pointing away, then a gate wire onto its far end.
  4. Solder a drain wire to the D leg.
  5. Tape everything except the S leg tip. Put a tape flag R / G / B on each module.
Wire: cut Freenove jumpers work well, since their colours help tell the gate wires apart. Leave tails long (10–12 cm); they get trimmed when everything goes into the channel. Do one module, send me a photo, then make the other two the same way.
