1. Unplug the charger. Solder short wires (the 24AWG from the MT3608 pack) to VIN+ and VIN−. VOUT can wait; you'll probe its pads directly.
  2. Connect VIN+ → CORD+ (plain white) and VIN− → CORD− (black). A temporary twist is fine for now; tape the two joints apart.
  3. Meter on DC volts: red probe on the VOUT+ pad, black on VOUT−. Nothing else touches VOUT.
  4. Plug the charger in and note the reading. It could be anything up to about 28 V.
  5. With a small flat screwdriver, turn the brass screw clockwise about 20 turns. It clicks at the end and never stops hard. The reading should fall to about 5 V.
  6. Now turn it anticlockwise slowly, half a turn at a time, until it reads 24.0 V. Never above 24.5 V.
  7. Unplug. Put a small piece of tape over the screw so it can't be knocked.
If the voltage moves the opposite way to the arrows, believe the meter. Your board's pad labels win over this drawing: VIN is on the coil side, VOUT on the screw side.
