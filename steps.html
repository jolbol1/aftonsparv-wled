IKEA lamp — one USB cable

  1. 1
    Photograph the IKEA board

    Remove the bottom cover (one screw is under the sticker). Photograph the wiring before you desolder anything.

  2. 2
    Flash WLED onto a C3

    install.wled.me in Chrome → ESP32-C3. LED Preferences: PWM RGB on GPIO 4, 5, 6. Wi-Fi TX power ≈ 8.5 dBm.

  3. 3
    Cut a USB-A cable and meter itneeds multimeter

    Plug it into the charger. Red should read +5 V against black, with no minus sign. Leave green and white unconnected.

  4. 4
    Set the MT3608 to 24.0 V

    Turn the pot clockwise about 20 turns first (down to about 5 V), then slowly anticlockwise until OUT reads 24.0 V with no load.

  5. 5
    Build the MOSFET stage

    GPIO → 100 Ω → gate, with 10 kΩ from gate to GND. Sources to GND. Drains to the LED's black (R), green (G) and transparent (B) wires. LED white to 24 V.

  6. 6
    Wire one colour, test, then the rest

    Smooth dimming from 0–100% means a real IRLZ44N. Dim or dead means a fake: swap it.

  7. 7
    Tape, fit, close

    The MT3608 is 14 mm tall. If it won't fit in the base, put it in a small box on the cable.

    ⚠ Never plug the C3's USB into your Mac while the lamp's 5 V is connected.
Lamp: one cable. All grounds are shared. Each MOSFET gets a 10 kΩ resistor from gate to GND.

Govee H6056 — serial investigation

  1. 1
    Note the versionsdone

    Firmware 1.10.02.

  2. 2
    Open the controller and fit headers

    Solder pins into 3V3 TX RX GND and GND RST CLK DAT 3V3. Photograph both sides of the board.

  3. 3
    Continuity trace, power offneeds multimeter

    Trace the USB-C output's data pins and the 4 AO3400 gates: which chip do they reach?

    → Realtek RTL8710BN
    Your own firmware on the Realtek is realistic (LibreTiny / ESPHome).
    → Bluetooth FR8018HA
    The Realtek is only a bridge: your firmware talks to the BLE chip, or you replace the controller.
  4. 4
    Back up the Realtek flash

    CP2102 + ltchiptool. Power the board from the Govee 12 V adapter. Hold the board's TX to GND at power-on, then release it. Read the full 4 MB.

    ⚠ Keep the dump private: it may contain your Wi-Fi password.
  5. 5
    Read the dump

    Run strings on the dump. Effect names mean the Realtek drives the LEDs. A command table means it's a bridge.

  6. 6
    Choose the firmware path

    Send me the photos and trace results, then we write it.

Serial: cross TX and RX, share GND. The board runs from its own 12 V adapter, not the CP2102.
